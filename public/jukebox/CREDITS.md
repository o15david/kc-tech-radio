# Wasteland Jukebox track credits and license verification

Every track below was verified individually before airing (David's
rule, Nov 20, 2026: verified public-domain or freely licensed
recordings only, each track's status checked before it airs).

Basis: under the U.S. Music Modernization Act's schedule for
pre-1972 sound recordings, recordings published before 1923 are in
the public domain, and recordings published 1923-1946 carry a
100-year term, so recordings from 1923, 1924, and 1925 entered the
public domain in 2024, 2025, and 2026 respectively. The underlying
compositions are likewise pre-1926 publications, public domain
under the 95-year term for published works. All eight recordings
below were published 1920-1925, verified against the Internet
Archive item metadata (recording date) on Nov 20, 2026.

Source: Internet Archive 78rpm collections (archive.org/details/78rpm
and the Great 78 Project items), downloaded Nov 20, 2026 and
re-encoded to 128 kbps mono MP3 for hosting size; audio content
unchanged.
