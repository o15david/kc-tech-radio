# Jingle bed credits

The music bed under the KBUNKC jingles (jingle-1.mp3 through
jingle-6.mp3, excerpt in bed-tiger-rag.mp3) is:

"Tiger Rag" — Original Dixieland Jass Band, Victor 18472-B, recorded
1918. Public domain. Source: Wikimedia Commons, "File:Tiger Rag
ODJB.ogg" (LicenseShortName: Public domain).

The chant voices are the station's own Edge TTS story voice
(en-US-ChristopherNeural) reading hand-written lines from the JINGLES
pool in lib/gonzo.js, mixed over the bed by
scripts/render-jingles.mjs (Route 1, approved Nov 20, 2026).
